Same start, same destination, for everyone on Earth today. Find the best journey: cheap, fast, and decided quickly.

How it works

  1. Build a route from the start city to the destination. Add stopovers if they make the trip cheaper or faster.
  2. Pick a transport for every leg: ✈️ flight, 🚆 train, 🚌 bus, 🚢 ferry, 🚗 car, 🚲 bike, 🚶 walk. Prices and times differ every day.
  3. Submit once. Your Traverse Score weighs money spent, hours travelled and how fast you decided. The clock starts when you press Start.
